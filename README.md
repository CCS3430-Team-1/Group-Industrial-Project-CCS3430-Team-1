WE LEAD Website Expansion: Requirements for Donation Pages
About this project
WE LEAD is an independent non-profit, founded in 2023 by Libra Philanthropies, that helps
women enter, grow and lead in tech and innovation in Greece. Our website, joinwelead.org,
is built on Joomla and maintained by SOUL.
We want to add a new page to the site:
● A donation page, so individuals can support our work online in a few clicks.
The page will be built separately from the Joomla site and linked from it. It should look and
feel like part of joinwelead.org, using our fonts, colours and logo, which we'll provide. We'll
launch in English first and add Greek if there's capacity, so please build the page ready for
translation.
Each section lists must-have and nice-to-have features. If time is limited, please deliver the
must-haves first. Decisions still open on our side are marked as Open decisions and won't
block the start of the work.
1. Donation page
Purpose: let individuals make a one-oƯ or monthly donation to support WE LEAD's work,
quickly and securely. It replaces our current donate button, which links to a file with our
bank details.
Audience: individuals. Corporate donations and sponsorships are out of scope for now.
Must-have
 Payment methods: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and IRIS, through a single
payment provider. Payments go through the provider's secure checkout; no card
data stored on our servers.
 Amounts: preset buttons for €5, €20 and €50, plus an "Other amount" field.
 One-oƯ & recurring donations / Individual + Corporate donations
 Donor details: name and email, plus an optional, unticked checkbox: "Keep me
updated about WE LEAD's news and programs."
 Bank transfer option: our IBAN shown on the page for donors who prefer it.
 Confirmation: a thank-you page and/or a confirmation email after a successful
donation.
 Content: a short "Why donate" text and one or two impact numbers, provided by
WE LEAD.
 Notifications: an email to a WE LEAD inbox for each donation (name, email,
amount, date).
 Admin list: a simple password-protected list of donations, including newsletter
opt-ins, with CSV export.
 Privacy: donations are private, with no public donor list.
Open decisions
 Payment provider: Viva or Stripe. Viva is our current preference because it
covers cards, Apple/Google Pay and IRIS in one integration. We'll confirm the
choice.
 Receipts: how receipts will be handled
 Newsletter connection: whether opt-ins connect directly to our newsletter
platform or are exported via CSV.
